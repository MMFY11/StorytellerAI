Once upon a time in the Enchanted Forest, there lived a prince named Evergreen, known far and wide not just for his wisdom but for his striking green hair, more vibrant than any forest foliage. The prince loved the woodland and spent his days exploring its mystical corners with Twinkle, his curious fox friend. One sunny morning, as rays of sunlight danced upon dew-kissed leaves, Twinkle tugged at Evergreen’s black-suited sleeve, itching for adventure.
