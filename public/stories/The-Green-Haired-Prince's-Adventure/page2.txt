As they wandered deeper, they stumbled upon Moonbeam Glade, where Lady Moonbeam, the gentle fairy, awaited. “There’s a music to be heard beneath the sea of trees,” she whispered, her lavender hair fluttering. Intrigued, Prince Evergreen followed her milky light through the shadowy brambles, surprisingly encountering Sir Barkstone. “Ah, young prince! Seek not the origin, but the story told by the sound,” he sang heartily, planting a paw reassuringly on Evergreen’s shoulder.
