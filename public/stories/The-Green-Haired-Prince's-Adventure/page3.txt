Drawn by the melody, they arrived at Barkstone Meadow, where the forest folk gathered, buzzing with tales and laughter. In awe, Evergreen realized the true treasure was every being and every whisper of life around him. He marveled at the unity and harmony, grateful for the unique friendships forged within the melody of nature. As stars began to twinkle and the moon graced the sky, the prince vowed to cherish his beloved forest forevermore.

And so the tale of Prince Evergreen and his companions became a cherished story of the Enchanted Forest, retold by moonlight and birdsong through the ages.
