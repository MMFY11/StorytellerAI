The tiger, with a mighty glare, decided against a confrontation with the steadfast watchdog and slinked back into the shadows of the forest. The flock cheered with bleats of relief, surrounding Max and nuzzling against him in gratitude.

The farmer, who had watched from afar, approached and praised Max's heroism, placing a garland of wildflowers around his neck—a crown worthy of the bravest. That night, beneath a blanket of twinkling stars, the farm returned to its usual serenity, knowing they had a hero among them—Max, the brave dog who saved his sheep.
