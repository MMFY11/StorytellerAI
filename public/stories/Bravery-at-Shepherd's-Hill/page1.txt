In a peaceful valley surrounded by rolling green hills and dotted with vibrant wildflowers, there was a farm known as Shepherd's Hill. The farm was a happy place, home to a kind farmer and his loyal dog named Max. Max was a handsome Collie—a medium-sized dog with a shiny, fluffy coat of white and rich sable, and bright, intelligent eyes that glowed like caramel. His nose was black as a coal, and his tail was a grand plume that waved like a flag. Max spent his days guiding a flock of fluffy, snowy-white sheep with wool as soft as clouds gently drifting in the sky.
