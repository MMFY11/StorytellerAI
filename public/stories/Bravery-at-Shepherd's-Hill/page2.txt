One sunlit morning, the sheep were happily grazing in the meadow, unaware of the cautiously creeping shadows in the forest behind them. The forest was a thick tangle of ancient trees with towering trunks and lush, leafy canopies. Emerging slowly from the shadows was a tiger, his stripes as bold and dark as the night against a fiery orange coat. His golden eyes gleamed like flickering lanterns, and his paws landed silently on the soft earth.
