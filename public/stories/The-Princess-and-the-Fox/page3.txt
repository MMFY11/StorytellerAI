Together, Princess Aurelia and Rusty discovered that their reflections in the pond could communicate with them by forming shapes and patterns in the water. Together, they learned the pond held the secret of the Kingdom's harmony—a secret that lay in the unity of colors and shapes their reflections formed.

That day, Princess Aurelia and Rusty made a promise; each time they felt alone or unsure, they would return to the pond for its wisdom. Thus, their bond grew stronger, as did their connection to the natural wonders around them. And so, the kingdom rejoiced, knowing the Princess and the Fox were guardians of light and friendship.
