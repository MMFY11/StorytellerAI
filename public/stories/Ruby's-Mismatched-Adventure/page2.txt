Beyond her usual stomping grounds, Ruby stumbled upon an unexpected sight—a cheerful family of yellow ducks. Each duckling in the family showcased fluffy plumage as bright as sunshine, illuminating the water with their cheerful hue. Their eyes twinkled like little stars, and they waddled in a rhythm matching the laughter of the wind.

The surroundings were vivid with lilies blooming pink and white, floating serenely on the tranquil pond's surface. Beneath a laneway of weeping willows, framed by their cascading green tendrils, Ruby found herself enchanted, joining their frolic without realizing they were not her own family.

The mama duck, with feathers the color of ripe lemons, had a gentle face that radiated kindness. She welcomed Ruby with a warm quack, surprising Ruby with her friendliness. Ruby felt a flutter of joy and played along happily.
