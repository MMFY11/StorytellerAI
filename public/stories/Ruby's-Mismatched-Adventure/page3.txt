As the day waned and the sky painted itself in hues of purple and amber, Ruby heard a familiar call. It was her own family of black ducks, their dark plumage mirrored the velvet blanket of the approaching night sky. They were searching for her, their voices carrying over the cool evening air. Ruby realized her mix-up, her small heart thudding with a blend of excitement and embarrassment.

Nestled in a cozy nook of cattails and water lilies, the black ducks welcomed Ruby back with open wings, where all was in harmony like the nightlife around them. Smiling, Ruby realized that although she might look different, she was loved in both places.

The story of Ruby's adventurous spirit and the accidental family swap quickly turned into a pond legend, affirming the idea of friendship beyond appearances and the joy of finding where one truly belongs.
