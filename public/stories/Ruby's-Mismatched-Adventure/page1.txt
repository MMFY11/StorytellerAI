Once upon a time, in a colorful, sparkling pond where the reflection of the sun danced upon the water, there lived a curious little black duck named Ruby. Ruby's feathers were as dark as midnight, glistening with a hint of mystery and magic. Her eyes shone like polished stones, revealing her adventurous spirit. Unlike the typical feathery tufts around her fellow ducks, Ruby's wings had a sleek sheen, making her stand out among the waterfowl.

One bright morning, Ruby waddled along with her pond friends, her black feathers contrasting with the golden reeds that swayed with the gentle breeze. She loved playing with her fellow ducklings, diving and splashing in the sun-drenched water. But that day, Ruby decided to venture a little further than usual.
