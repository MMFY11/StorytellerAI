As they played, Ruby and Benny heard a soft cry for help. They followed the sound and found a hedgehog named Sally tangled in some vines. Sally was covered in brown, spiky quills, but her face was gentle and worried. "Can you help me, please?" she asked, with her big, watery eyes filled with hope.

Ruby and Benny knew they had to help. Using her trunk, Ruby gently untangled Sally from the vines. Sally thanked them with a big smile and offered them sweet berries from her basket as a gesture of gratitude. These berries were ruby red, just like Ruby, and tasted simply delightful.
