Once upon a time, in the vibrant jungle of Timbali, there lived a very special elephant named Ruby. Unlike the other elephants who were grey, Ruby stood out with her brilliant red skin that shimmered in the sunlight. Her ears were large and floppy, perfect for flapping away flies, and her tail was swishy and long with a tuft of white hair at the end. Ruby had big, friendly eyes that were full of curiosity and joy.

One day, Ruby set out to explore the jungle with her best friend, Benny the Bluebird. Benny was a small bird with soft blue feathers and a cheerful tune that lifted everyone's spirits. Together, they traveled to the Great Banana Grove, where they liked to play hide and seek among the towering green banana plants.
