Feeling proud of their good deed, Ruby, Benny, and Sally decided to continue their adventure together. They ventured to the Silver Waterfall, a breathtaking sight where cool, sparkling water cascaded down shiny grey rocks into a clear blue pool. The sunlight danced on the water, creating a rainbow of colors.

As they rested by the waterfall, they promised to always help those in need and keep exploring their beautiful jungle home. Beneath the sky that turned a soft pink as the day ended, Ruby, Benny, and Sally knew they had a friendship that was as colorful and unique as they were.

From that day forward, everyone in Timbali knew about Ruby the Red Elephant and her heart as bright and warm as her extraordinary color. And so, their adventures continued, surrounded by the wonders of the jungle they adored.
